Trusted-person local model notice
=================================

This app bundles these local inference assets solely for the explicitly
requested trusted-person prototype. They are never downloaded at runtime and
camera photos are not sent to a server.

1. face_landmarker.task
   Source: Google MediaPipe model distribution
   URL: https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/face_landmarker/face_landmarker/float16/1/face_landmarker.task
   SHA-256: 64184e229b263107bc2b804c6625db1341ff2bb731874b0bcc2fe6544e0bc9ff
   Used for: one-face validation and blink-coefficient measurement.
   License: Apache License 2.0. MediaPipe sample source is Apache-2.0.

2. mobilefacenet.tflite
   Source: hugocornellier/face_detection_tflite, commit 50c784adaa9f40c722affb1d4412674f25e1fe0c
   URL: https://github.com/hugocornellier/face_detection_tflite
   SHA-256: be4bc7cfc53f7bc336d0f28b1ab92535f618c913a422b683210750f6b5354854
   Used for: a 192-value, normalized local face template after explicit consent.
   License: Apache License 2.0 (repository LICENSE; its model documentation
   states the included TFLite assets are Apache-2.0 licensed).

Neither asset is a presentation-attack detector. A blink movement check is a
basic interaction signal, not proof of identity or liveness. Do not use it for
financial authentication, medical decisions, emergency access, surveillance,
or high-risk identification.
